Our Story & Craft

Born from Ember, Steeped in Leaf

Ember & Leaf began with a quiet conviction: a remarkable cup should feel like an unhurried ritual, never rushed and never ordinary.

Every morning, we calibrate our roasts for velvet crema and sweet caramel depth while steeping whole black, green, and floral tea leaves at exact temperatures. Whether you reach for a bold Vietnamese phin brew or a cloud-soft Hokkaido milk tea, every pour is freshly prepared with Madagascar vanilla, roasted Okinawa kokuto sugar, stone-milled Uji matcha, and pure cocoa.
